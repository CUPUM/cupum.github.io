On November 26, 2026, Chairholder Shin Koseki will speak online at the Local Governance and Partnership Forum of the 10th Regional Symposium on Effective Governance and AI Transformation, organized by the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs (UN DESA) through its Division for Public Institutions and Digital Government and its United Nations Project Office on Governance (UNPOG). The Symposium takes place in Incheon, Republic of Korea, and online from November 25 to 27, co-organized with the Ministry of the Interior and Safety of the Republic of Korea and Incheon Metropolitan City.

His talk, "Governing AI from the Local Context: Where, and Whether, AI is Appropriate", is part of the session "AI and Local Governance: Empowering Local Governments through Institutional Capacity, Service Delivery Innovation and Cooperation". The annual Symposium is a regional platform for policy dialogue, peer learning and cooperation among senior government officials, international organizations, civil society, the private sector and academia from Asia-Pacific and beyond.

The forum takes place on November 27, 11:30 to 12:30 Seoul time (November 26, 21:30 to 22:30 Montréal time). Follow the Symposium online on the UNPOG website: www.unpog.org
