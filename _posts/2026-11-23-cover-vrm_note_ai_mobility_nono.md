Eddy Damaris Nono Defo, Master's student in environment and sustainable development at Université de Montréal and research assistant at the UNESCO Chair in Urban Landscape, has published a research note in the "Place à la relève !" section of the Villes Régions Monde (VRM) network: ["Participation citoyenne dans la gouvernance de l'intelligence artificielle appliquée à la mobilité urbaine : cas de la région métropolitaine de Montréal"](https://www.vrm.ca/participation-citoyenne-dans-la-gouvernance-de-lintelligence-artificielle-appliquee-a-la-mobilite-urbaine-cas-de-la-region-metropolitaine-de-montreal/). Mobility is one of the sectors most transformed by AI, yet the policies that frame these tools remain largely technocratic and leave little room for the citizens who use them every day.

Drawing on 24 semi-structured interviews with government actors, experts, organizations and citizens of the Montreal metropolitan region, Eddy maps who takes part in governing AI for urban mobility and what can strengthen citizen involvement. Three levers stand out: participation formats that go beyond traditional public consultations, the inclusion of marginalized groups, and digital education to reduce the knowledge gap between experts and non-experts.

He concludes that citizens should be involved from the ideation phase of a project through to its implementation, because their lived experience is knowledge that makes solutions work. The note proposes an inclusive governance model bringing together citizens, experts, organizations and government actors.
