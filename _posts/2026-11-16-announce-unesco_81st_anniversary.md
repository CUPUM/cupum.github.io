On November 16, 1945, representatives of 37 countries meeting in London signed the Constitution of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization. UNESCO was founded on the conviction that peace must be built "in the minds of men and women" through education, science, culture and communication.

Eighty-one years later, UNESCO brings together 194 Member States. From the World Heritage Convention and the Recommendation on the Historic Urban Landscape to the Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence, its normative work shapes how cities, landscapes and new technologies are understood and governed around the world. Through the UNITWIN/UNESCO Chairs Programme, it also connects some 950 UNESCO Chairs in about 120 countries.

Since 2003, the UNESCO Chair in Urban Landscape at Université de Montréal has contributed to this mission by building bridges between the University, UNESCO, its partner organizations and international stakeholders. Through action-research and research-creation, including the UNESCO Studio and the Fleuves en communs initiative, the Chair works with communities, students and partners on the design of just, green and democratic urban landscapes. On this anniversary, we are proud to be among the intellectual partners of UNESCO and to share in its mission.
