Leandry Jieutsa, PhD candidate at the UNESCO Chair in Urban Landscape, has published "Ecosystem factors influencing the use and governance of AI in municipalities" in the Journal of Smart Cities and Society (vol. 5, no. 3, 2026). The open-access article examines how the broader ecosystem of actors shapes the deployment of artificial intelligence in Quebec municipalities.

Drawing on press coverage, municipal documents, interviews and network mapping, the study shows that AI adoption is strongly shaped by structural power asymmetries and by a partial outsourcing of governance to technology firms and intermediary organizations. While flexible coordination supports experimentation, the lack of operational oversight mechanisms creates risks of technocentric governance.

The article calls for stronger municipal governance capacity and ecosystem-informed regulatory frameworks to enable equitable and responsible urban AI, a contribution at the heart of the Chair's work on AI and cities. [Read the article](https://doi.org/10.1177/27723577261447823).
