The UNESCO Chair in Urban Landscape is pleased to welcome Damian Siqueiros, artist-researcher and founder and creative director of the Terra Sapiens Collective, an artistic laboratory dedicated to eco-socially sustainable art practices that brings together Western science, Indigenous knowledge, environmental justice and circular design. A multidisciplinary artist working across photography, video, multimedia installation and art direction, he has taken part in more than 60 exhibitions internationally, including at the Art Museum of the Americas, the Frost Museum in Miami and the Carrousel du Louvre, and has led over 40 Art for Impact workshops in Asia, Europe and North America since 2018. He is currently pursuing a PhD in arts studies and practices at UQAM, co-supervised by Laura Pereira (Stockholm Resilience Centre), where he deepens the theoretical foundations of his Art for Impact Framework.

His arrival comes with great news: the Terra Sapiens Collective has received funding from the Canada Council for the Arts, through its Artistic Creation component, for Fleuves en Communs: Building New Ecosocial Imaginaries Through Art for the Conservation of the Saint Lawrence River. Co-led with artist Alyssa Martens in partnership with the Chair, this transdisciplinary project of photography, film, poetry and storytelling pairs scientists, fishers, seafarers and Indigenous knowledge holders with artists who turn their testimonies into stories, poems and dances. It will culminate in an immersive installation of about 40 minutes, whose final chapter will draw on a participatory foresight workshop with artists, scientists and riverside communities. The project also brings together Wildlife Conservation Society Canada, the Indigenous Curatorial Collective, ECOMARIS and the Stockholm Resilience Centre, through the INSPIRI network.

Congratulations, Damian, and welcome to the team! We acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for the Arts.
