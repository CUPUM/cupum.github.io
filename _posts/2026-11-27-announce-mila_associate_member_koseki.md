The Scientific Council of Mila – Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute has approved the appointment of Shin Koseki, holder of the UNESCO Chair in Urban Landscape, as an Associate Academic Member of Mila, for a three-year term from November 2026 to November 2029. The appointment changes his category of membership and renews his affiliation with the institute.

Mila is a community of more than 1,200 researchers specializing in machine learning, and one of the world's leading academic centres for artificial intelligence. The appointment recognizes the Chair's work at the intersection of AI, citizen participation, urban planning and landscape design, including research on urban AI governance and participatory tools for public space design such as WeDesign+.

As an Associate Academic Member, Shin will continue to collaborate with the Mila community through joint research, co-supervision and events, strengthening links between AI research and the design of more inclusive cities. We are honoured by this recognition and look forward to the collaborations it will open for the Chair's students and partners.
