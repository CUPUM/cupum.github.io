On October 20, 2026, Chairholder Shin Koseki gave a lecture titled "Rivers in Commons: Rethinking Continental Waterways for Urban Sustainable Development in a One Health World" at Cairo University. Hosted by the Faculty of Urban and Regional Planning, the lecture was given to its professors and students in preparation for the UNESCO Studio Cairo, which will take place on the Nile in June 2027. It introduced Rivers in Commons, the Chair's research initiative on improving life along the world's great rivers for ecosystems, economies and well-being.

Starting from a meeting with a Wolastoqiyik former park ranger on the shores of the St. Lawrence, Shin Koseki showed how knowledge about rivers gets lost between universities, institutions and residents, and how it can be recovered by going to the field and listening to the "connectors" who link these worlds. He drew on four rivers studied by the UNESCO Studio since 2021, the Red River in Hanoi, the Mekong in Phnom Penh, the Mississippi in New Orleans and the St. Lawrence, before turning to the Nile, and presented the One Health approach, which treats the health of people, animals, plants and rivers as interconnected.

We thank the Faculty of Urban and Regional Planning at Cairo University for its warm welcome, and look forward to working with its students and professors on the Nile in 2027.
